Questions

Is the AI receptionist really free?

Yes, for founding pros: everyone who claims it before Scoo opens in their area. A fair-use limit covers normal call volumes for a local business, and we'll tell you what it is before it switches on.

What does it cost?

The AI receptionist is free for founding pros. When Scoo opens in your area, you can also choose to receive new customer calls from Scoo. Those are pay-per-call: you'll see the price before you take one, and you set your own limits.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. Keep your number. You forward the calls you can't answer to Scoo, and it picks up from there.

When does Scoo start in my area?

City by city, once enough customers and pros in an area have signed up. You'll hear from us before it opens near you.

Are these leads shared with other pros?

Each call goes to one pro at a time. If you don't pick up, it moves to the next pro, so the customer still gets help fast.

Can I choose my hours and area?

Yes. You set the ZIP codes you cover and when you're available, and you can pause any time.
